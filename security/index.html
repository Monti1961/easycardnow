Security

Security guidance focused on reducing user mistakes and avoiding impersonation.

Never share secrets

Never share passwords, recovery phrases, private keys or one-time authentication codes.

Verify channels

Check that you are communicating through the official channel before providing personal or payment information.

Check crypto transfers

Verify address, network and amount before confirming a transfer.

Protect documents

Submit KYC documents only through the intended verification process.
