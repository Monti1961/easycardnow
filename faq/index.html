FAQ

Is EasyCardNow the card issuer?

EasyCardNow provides information and a user journey around supported services. Do not assume that EasyCardNow itself is the issuer unless the applicable legal documentation expressly says so.

Is KYC required?

It may be, depending on the service, jurisdiction and provider requirements.

Can I top up with USDT?

Where supported, follow the current instructions and verify network, address, amount and payment window.

Are fees fixed?

No. Applicable fees and limits can change and should be checked before each transaction.

Is a transaction reversible?

Crypto transfers are generally difficult or impossible to reverse once confirmed.

Where do I get support?

Use the official support channel presented by the service you are using. Be cautious of impersonators.
