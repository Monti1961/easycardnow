KYC & Identity Verification

Clear information about identity checks without promising approval or a particular verification outcome.

Why KYC may be required

Card and payment services may need to verify identity to meet applicable legal, compliance, fraud-prevention or provider requirements.

What may be requested

  • Identity document details
  • Basic personal information
  • Proof-of-address or additional information where required
  • Selfie or liveness verification in some flows

Protect yourself

Only submit documents through the official verification flow. Do not send passport images, passwords, recovery phrases or one-time codes to random accounts.

Verification outcome

Completion of KYC does not guarantee card issuance. A provider may decline or request additional information.
