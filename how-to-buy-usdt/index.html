How to Buy USDT

Educational guidance for acquiring USDT through a reputable exchange or wallet before a supported card top-up.

General steps

  1. Choose a reputable, legally available exchange or wallet.
  2. Complete any identity verification required by that provider.
  3. Buy USDT using a supported payment method.
  4. Confirm the blockchain network before sending USDT elsewhere.
  5. Keep transaction records and never share recovery phrases or private keys.
