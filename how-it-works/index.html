How It Works

A simple, transparent journey designed to help you understand each step before money is moved.

01 — Discover

Read product, eligibility, fees and risk information.

02 — Onboard

Enter the supported Telegram or web flow and review consent.

03 — Verify

Complete KYC if the applicable provider requires it.

04 — Select

Choose an available card option and review its terms.

05 — Fund

Use a supported funding method and verify every transaction detail.

06 — Support

Track status and use the official support route for issues.
