How to Connect a Virtual Card to Apple Pay and Google Pay

A practical guide to mobile payments, card eligibility and starting the virtual-card connection process.

What are Apple Pay and Google Pay?

Apple Pay and Google Pay allow users to make contactless or online payments with a compatible card added to a supported device. The exact features depend on the country, device, card type and card issuer.

How to add a card

Apple Pay

  1. Open the Wallet app on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the add-card button.
  3. Follow the on-screen instructions and complete any verification required by the issuer.
  4. Wait for confirmation that the card has been added.

Google Pay / Google Wallet

  1. Open Google Wallet on a compatible Android device.
  2. Choose to add a payment card.
  3. Enter the card details or follow the supported setup flow.
  4. Complete issuer verification if requested.

How to start connecting an EasyCardNow card

The EasyCardNow Telegram bot is the entry point for starting the card application and onboarding process. Use the bot to review the available options and follow the instructions for connecting or ordering a supported virtual card.

Before attempting to add the card to Apple Pay or Google Pay, check whether the specific card and issuer support the relevant mobile wallet. Adding a card is not guaranteed solely because it is virtual.
